LINK F.L.A.M is a trusted bridge between people who need work done and freelancers who do it well, and every project helps build stronger communities.

How linking works

  1. Seekers choose. Browse the directory and send a booking request with your project brief.
  2. LINK checks. We contact the freelancer to confirm they are available for your project.
  3. LINK connects. Once confirmed, we introduce you both so the work can begin.
100%

Fair pay promise

Freelancers keep 100% of their quoted price. LINK adds a transparent Linking Fee on top for the Seeker, so nothing is taken from the person doing the work.

The Green Fever Project

The Linking Fee directly funds LINK's Green Fever Project, an initiative to reduce unemployment, uplift public spaces and advance communities.
