Privacy Notice

This notice explains how GET LINK (PTY) LTD ("LINK", "we", "us") handles your personal information on LINK F.L.A.M, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

If you send a booking request (Seekers):

  • Your name, company (if given), email address and phone number
  • Your project brief, and your preferred date and time

If you apply to be listed (Freelancers):

  • Private details: your full name, phone number, email address and standard rates
  • Optional: a portfolio link and/or portfolio document, to help us review your application
  • Public profile details: display name, industry, short service description, location, work style and profile photo

Why we collect it

  • To confirm a freelancer's availability and connect Seekers and Freelancers
  • To review applications and build the public marketplace
  • To reply to you and send confirmations

Who sees it

  • Your private details (and any portfolio link or document you send) are seen only by the LINK team.
  • A Freelancer's public profile details (display name, photo, industry, description, location and work style) are shown on the marketplace once approved.
  • A Seeker's contact details and brief are shared with the chosen Freelancer only so that LINK can connect you both.
  • We use trusted service providers to run the website and send emails. They process information on our behalf and may store it outside South Africa.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. Freelancers can ask us at any time to remove their public profile.

Your rights

You may ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may object to how we use it. To do so, message us on WhatsApp at +27 74 571 8332.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.
